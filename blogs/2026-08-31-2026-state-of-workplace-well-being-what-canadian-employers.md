Canadian employees' well-being declined across nearly every dimension in 2026, with sleep, financial health, and mental health showing the sharpest drops compared to 2025. Nearly 8 in 10 employees say these challenges have affected their ability to do their job. Among employees with workplace health benefits, 67% used them in the past year, but only 20% were very satisfied with the experience — and only 31% of HR leaders receive regular, clear data from their benefits provider to prove those programs are working.
